# Closing an app presses Force stop in Settings

When the owner closes an app in the App Switcher or the System Monitor, the app must really end. `ActivityManager.killBackgroundProcesses`, the only way a regular app can end another, wasn't enough on the owner's TV: it ends only service and cached processes, and apps with services restart by themselves right after (the owner saw "Fechar" do nothing on 2026-10-24). Force stop ends every process and keeps the app from restarting until it is opened again, but Android grants it only to the system (`FORCE_STOP_PACKAGES`), and Setup can't grant that permission.

So the Home presses the button the owner would: the key filter (ADR 0005) already holds accessibility access, and with window content retrieval it opens the app's page in Settings, presses Force stop, confirms with OK and backs out of Settings to where the owner was. It finds the button by Settings' own string (`device_apps_app_management_force_stop`, "Forçar parada" on the TV) and the confirmation by its title, and it presses Back only while Settings is in front. Settings shows for about a second; that was accepted over a close that silently fails.

Apps Android protects, playing media or with a foreground service, are never closed: the owner decided closing should leave them alone. The Open App Limit still closes quietly with `killBackgroundProcesses`, since an automatic close must never flash Settings. Settings itself is never an Open App: it runs the TV's Bluetooth services, comes right back when ended, and opening it to close an app would otherwise make it the most recently used app.
